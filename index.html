Remote-First Workflow

Intake to delivery in a structured asynchronous sequence

  1. 01

    Structured intake submission

    A prospective client submits business context and operational priorities details through the website intake form.

  2. 02

    Qualification and acknowledgement

    Submission receives automatic acknowledgement and fit review before proposal.

  3. 03

    Portal creation on engagement

    A private project portal is created for document exchange and delivery tracking.

  4. 04

    Asynchronous evidence upload

    The client uploads files, recordings, and process information for review.

  5. 05

    Analysis and report delivery

    Findings, process maps, and documentation are returned through the portal.

  6. 06

    Sign-off or retainer transition

    Work concludes with project sign-off or continuation under Clarity Partner.
